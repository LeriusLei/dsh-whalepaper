## Anatomy and behaviour references

- [NOAA Ocean Today: Killer Whale Anatomy](https://oceantoday.noaa.gov/killerwhaleanatomy/).
- [NOAA Fisheries: Killer Whale](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/killer-whale).
- [Wright et al.2017: Fine-scale foraging movements and prey escape](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5319153/).
- [WDC: vertical tail propulsion](https://uk.whales.org/whales-dolphins/why-do-whale-and-dolphin-tails-go-up-and-down/).

These informed qualitative anatomy and motion. Cadences and gravity are deliberately adjusted for a quiet wallpaper, not reported as measured biological values.
